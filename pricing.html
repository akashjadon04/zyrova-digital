Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you charge one-time instead of monthly?
We believe you should own your business assets. When you pay monthly for Wix or an agency retainer, you are renting. If you stop paying, your site disappears. With Zyrova, once the invoice is paid, you own the code, the domain, and the design forever.
How long does it take to launch?
Our "Fast Launch" package typically goes live in 7 days. The "Growth Engine" takes about 2-3 weeks depending on how quickly we receive your photos and content.
Will I be able to edit the website myself?
Yes. We can set up a headless CMS (Content Management System) that allows you to edit text, change prices, and swap photos easily without touching any code.
What about hosting and domain fees?
We recommend Netlify or Vercel for hosting, which are often free for small business sites, or very low cost ($0-$20/mo). Domain names (yourname.com) cost about $12/year, which you pay directly to the registrar.
Do you write the text/copy?
Yes! Our "Growth Engine" package includes professional copywriting optimized for conversion. For the "Fast Launch" package, we provide templates for you to fill out.
